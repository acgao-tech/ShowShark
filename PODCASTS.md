# Podcasts in ShowShark

ShowShark brings your favorite podcasts into the same self-hosted media experience as the rest of your library. Search for a podcast and subscribe from ShowShark Server, then browse and play its episodes from ShowShark Client. Both audio and video podcasts are supported, with media delivered through your ShowShark Server to your Apple devices.

Podcast subscriptions live alongside YouTube subscriptions in Channels. ShowShark imports the latest episodes from each podcast feed, keeps the subscription refreshed, and presents each podcast as a familiar show with artwork, seasons, episode details, and native playback controls.

## ShowShark Server

Manage podcasts from the **Channels** tab in ShowShark Server. Choose **Podcast Subscription** from the add menu, search by podcast name, and select the feed you want. You can also add a podcast directly from its RSS feed URL and choose how many existing episodes to import.

Before subscribing, ShowShark previews the selected feed so you can confirm its title, author, artwork, media type, and episode count. Once added, the podcast appears in **Podcast Subscriptions**, where you can see its refresh status and manage it alongside your other channel sources.

## ShowShark Client

Podcast subscriptions automatically appear in the **Podcasts** section of the Client's **Channels** tab. Select a podcast to browse its seasons and episodes, then open any episode to view its artwork, description, publish date, and duration.

Playback is streamed through ShowShark Server. Audio podcasts use a dedicated player with artwork, seeking, skip controls, and volume control, while video podcasts play through ShowShark's native video experience.
